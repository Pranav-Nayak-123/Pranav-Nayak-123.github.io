01 · About

Systems, not notebooks

I'm a final-year B.Tech (Hons.) Computer Science student at RV University. My first real project was getting a computer to follow a tennis ball. The ball is tiny, fast and blurred, and it kept vanishing behind players. Fixing that, by pairing a YOLOv8 detector with optical flow so the track survives the frames the detector misses, taught me what I still care about most: making a model hold up on messy, real input.

Since then I've built football analytics, a Braille-teaching device with real solenoids, a pipeline that turns a short story into a narrated video, and, with my team, a construction-site safety system that is live on the web. I'm also first author on a paper about reconstructing 3D objects from a single image.

I like knowing the numbers behind a claim, writing tests that keep a system honest, and shipping something people can actually open.

Live Team of 4 · CS3235

Ground Truth: Construction Safety Twin

Turns site CCTV into safety evidence: detection and tracking, eight hazard rules (PPE, zones, machinery, edges, worker down, suspended loads, fire and smoke), an interactive 3D digital twin, a 5×5 risk register, and a risk assistant that checks every claim against the run's facts.

My part: the CV pipeline (YOLOE-26l, tracking, PPE classifier, camera calibration), hazard rules R1–R5 and the video renderer.

  • 0.989person AP50 on held-out data
  • 0.39 → 0.85missing-helmet recall on unseen images
  • $0per month to host
